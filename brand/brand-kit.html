NIIP — National ICT Intelligence Program

The identity for the national program, the NIIS system that powers it, and the NICTD web application. Use this page for every logo, colour and type decision.

The mark

Three layers in one engine

The octagon is the shape of the NIIS engine. Inside it, three offset bands are the program's three physical layers, drawn as they appear when the system is pulled apart.

  1. Web Application

    The top band. NICTD, where users meet the data.

  2. NIIS, the engine

    The middle band is the brightest: the National ICT Intelligence System that powers the whole program.

  3. ICT Databases

    The bottom band. Indicators, reports and research.

  4. The red point

    Flag red, used once. It marks the output: intelligence leaving the system.

Lockup & spacing

Name beside the mark

Keep clear space equal to one band's height (the white bar) on every side. The smallest size is 24 px on screen or 8 mm in print; below that, use the mark alone.
